The club

The Panthers are Evandale's home for cricket. We field senior sides on Saturdays, run junior cricket for kids learning the game, and keep the clubrooms open for everyone who wants to be part of it.

Whether you've played for years, haven't picked up a bat since school, or just want to help out on game day, there's a spot for you. New players, families, scorers, umpires and volunteers are always welcome.

  • Senior cricketCompetitive and social grades every Saturday through the summer.
  • Junior cricketSkills, games and a safe, encouraging start for young players.
  • Club lifePresentation night, family days and a drink after stumps.
  • Get involvedScore, umpire, coach, run the BBQ or help with the ground.
